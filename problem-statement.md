Develop robust AI language technologies for Marwari, Mewari, Dhundhari, Hadoti, Mewati, and Bagri by collecting and validating high-quality speech and text datasets, benchmarking existing models, and enhancing Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), Text-to-Speech (TTS), and Machine Translation (MT) systems to enable accurate, context-aware, and multilingual interactions with English, Hindi, and other Indian languages.
1. Objectives
Benchmark and analyze existing AI translation models of text-to-text translation, from Marwari & Mewari to English and other regional languages of India and vice versa.
Collect and validate extensive voice and text data for Marwari, Mewari, Dhundhari, Hadoti, Mewati, and Bagri Dialects/Languages ensuring comprehensive linguistic cover.
Build/Train Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), Text-to-Speech (TTS), and Machine Translation (MT) models using newly curated datasets to enable seamless voice-based communication.
Implement NLP techniques to preserve linguistic nuances, idiomatic expressions, tone, and grammatical fidelity in translations and voice interactions.
Evaluate early prototypes using metrics like Word Error Rate (WER), Mean Opinion Score (MOS), and Use-Case Coverage to iteratively enhance accuracy and usability.
Encourage collaboration between AI researchers, linguists, and developers to advance regional language translation.
Improve the overall user experience by integrating advanced technologies like AI and ASR, ensuring high accuracy, real-time feedback, and seamless interaction.
